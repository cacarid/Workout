Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Wedding Prep Workout Tracker handles information when you use the application.

Information stored by the app

The app lets you record workouts, meals, water intake, and weight measurements. This information is stored locally in your web browser using browser storage. The current version does not send this information to a server or create an online user account.

WHOOP data

The current version of the app is not connected to WHOOP and does not access WHOOP account or health data. If a WHOOP integration is added in the future, this policy will be updated to explain what data is accessed, how it is used, and how it is stored.

Third-party services

The app may load fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser may send a request to Google to retrieve those fonts. No workout, meal, water, or weight entries are included in that request.

Data deletion

You can remove locally stored app data by clearing this website's browser storage through your browser settings. Because the current app does not send data to a server, there is no server-side account data to delete.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a new revision date.
